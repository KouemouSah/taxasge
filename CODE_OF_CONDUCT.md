# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

The Facil platform serves the citizens and government of Equatorial Guinea. All contributors and participants are expected to maintain the highest standards of professionalism, integrity, and respect.

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

- Professional and respectful communication
- Constructive feedback and collaboration
- Focus on the mission: serving citizens and government efficiency
- Data privacy and security consciousness
- Compliance with government regulations and standards

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or disrespectful conduct
- Unauthorized access to or disclosure of sensitive data
- Introduction of malicious code or security vulnerabilities
- Misuse of government data or systems
- Violation of the proprietary license terms

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:
- GitHub repository (issues, PRs, discussions)
- Code reviews and technical discussions
- Communication channels related to the project
- Public representation of the project

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to:
- **Email**: kouemou.sah@gmail.com

All reports will be reviewed and investigated. Violations may result in:
1. Warning
2. Temporary suspension of access
3. Permanent removal from the project
4. Legal action (for security violations or data breaches)
